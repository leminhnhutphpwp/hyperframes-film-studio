# Era and region templates

Every costume in the library is **symbolic**: it suggests a period through the shape of the hat, the cut of the
clothes and the kind of weapon. Nothing is modelled on a real dynasty or a real army. The `era` and `region` codes
below are used as tags.

## Eras (`era`)

| Code | Rough period | How to recognise it |
|---|---|---|
| `prehistoric` | Tribes, Stone Age | Animal hides, wooden bows, stone spears |
| `ancient` | Bronze Age – early Iron Age | Wrapped cloth garments, round shields, bronze spears, short swords |
| `medieval` | Feudal era | Scale or padded armour, helmets, bows, halberds, sabres |
| `early-modern` | Early firearms | Long-sleeved coats, conical hats, matchlock muskets, swords |
| `modern` | 19th – early 20th century | Tailored uniforms, hard caps, rifles |

## Regions (`region`)

| Code | Covers |
|---|---|
| `southeast-asia` | Southeast Asia |
| `east-asia` | East Asia |
| `steppe` | Steppe nomads |
| `middle-east` | Middle East, Persia |
| `europe` | Europe |
| `mediterranean` | The ancient Mediterranean |
| `south-asia` | South Asia |

## Roles (`role`)

`light-infantry`, `heavy-infantry`, `archer`, `cavalry`, `general`, `navy`, `civilian`.

## Faction colours

Kits are drawn with their faction cloth in teal `#2A8C8C`. `asset.py recolor` dyes it to each battle's faction colour
(red, yellow, blue, green, purple, white, black), so one kit works for every side.
